A methodology question — no dataset behind it:

$ARGUMENTS

1. Read `wiki/methods/`. If a standing method already covers this, the answer **is** that
   method, and the conversation is only about whether this case is an exception.
2. Ask `scholar` what the field does and what we have already read.
3. Interview the analyst on what is specific to this company — cost, spillover, what has
   been tried.
4. Propose a method page from `wiki/_templates/method.md`, and **wait**. Never establish
   a standing method alone; it binds every future question.
5. Amend an existing method rather than adding a near-duplicate. If genuinely superseded,
   keep the old page and mark it.
